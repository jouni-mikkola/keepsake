# Privacy

Keepsake doesn't collect or send any data. It has no servers, accounts, analytics or ads.

It notices video addresses in your open tabs so it can list them. That list stays in memory and is gone when you close the tab. Videos are downloaded from the same address the page was already using. Articles are read from the open page and stay on your computer.

## Permissions

Access to all sites lets it notice videos on any news site and read the article you want to save. Downloads opens the save dialog. Offscreen joins video pieces into one file. Storage keeps the video list while the tab is open.

Questions go to the [issues page](https://github.com/jouni-mikkola/keepsake/issues).
